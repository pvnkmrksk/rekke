# Code of Conduct

Rekkefly follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be welcoming and kind, assume good faith, give and accept
constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people who
use Rekkefly in their research. Harassment, insults and personal or political
attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other
project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer
([@pvnkmrksk](https://github.com/pvnkmrksk)) privately, using the contact options
on their GitHub profile. You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on
any comment. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, code and issues that break
this code, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
